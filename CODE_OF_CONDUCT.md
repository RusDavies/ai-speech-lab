# Code Of Conduct

AI Speech Lab uses a simple conduct standard:

- Be respectful and precise.
- Assume good faith until there is evidence not to.
- Criticize ideas, code, evidence, and trade-offs rather than people.
- Do not harass, threaten, demean, or deliberately exclude people.
- Do not post private voice samples, identity-sensitive audio, credentials, or
  private personal information.

Maintainers may remove issues, comments, pull requests, or other contributions
that violate these expectations. Repeated or severe violations may lead to
being blocked from participating in the project.

Security or privacy-sensitive concerns should follow `SECURITY.md`.
